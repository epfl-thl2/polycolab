# Workspaces and access

A workspace gives related research a shared home and an explicit access boundary.

## Browse public workspaces

Use [Library](/library) to search public research or open the [public workspace directory](/workspaces?scope=public).
Anyone can read public research without signing in.

## Work in your own workspace

After signing in, open [Workspaces](/workspaces) to create a workspace or return to one where you are a member.
Owners manage membership and workspace settings.
Contributors can add research where their role permits it.

## Understand visibility

- Public workspace research is readable by everyone.
- Private workspace research is limited to its members.
- Personal notes and conversations are not shared research.
- An external agent follows the current workspace memberships and roles of the account that created its key.

See [Workspace setup](../setup/workspace.md) for creation and membership tasks.
