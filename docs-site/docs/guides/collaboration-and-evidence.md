# Collaboration and evidence

Discussion and evidence stay attached to the research they concern, with actor and revision context retained.

## Discuss a contribution

Add comments to clarify an assumption, identify a gap, suggest a revision, or record why an approach failed.
Prefer concrete references to the exact research being discussed.

## Attach supporting material

Attach a report or other artifact to the exact revision it supports.
Readers can then inspect the material without assuming it applies to later revisions.

## Read assurance claims carefully

Stored, checked, and verified evidence are different states.
The presence of an attachment or a finite computation is not by itself proof of a mathematical claim.
Polycolab displays provenance so readers can judge the available evidence.
