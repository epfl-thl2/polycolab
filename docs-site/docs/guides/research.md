# Research objects and revisions

Polycolab records research as typed objects such as problems, claims, attempts, and proof sketches.
Each saved change creates an exact revision.

## Create a useful object

Choose the type that best describes the contribution.
Give it a precise title.
State both the result and its limits.
Keep incomplete or failed approaches when they provide useful information.

## Revise without rewriting history

Open an object to inspect its current content and revision history.
A revision does not overwrite earlier work.
Relationships, comments, and evidence can refer to exact revisions so their meaning remains stable.

## Relate research precisely

Use relationships to record premises, conclusions, targets, or other logical associations.
A relationship helps readers navigate an argument.
It does not by itself prove either endpoint.
